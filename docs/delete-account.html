Delete Your Stedfast Account

Last updated: July 18, 2026

Stedfast is an intermittent fasting and macro tracking app developed by Shriram M S. You can permanently delete your Stedfast account and all associated data directly within the app — no need to contact anyone.

How to Request Deletion

To delete your account and all your data:

  1. Open the Stedfast app and log in to your account.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete account near the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.
This is immediate: once you confirm, your account and data are deleted right away. You will be signed out automatically.

What Data Is Deleted

Confirming deletion permanently removes:

  • Your account credentials (name, email address, password)
  • All logged fasting sessions and fasting schedules
  • All saved dishes, meals, and meal logs
  • All body stats (height, weight, and other measurements you logged)
  • Your calorie and macronutrient intake limits and daily intake summaries

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered, and you would need to create a new account to use Stedfast again.

What Data Is Retained

We do not retain any of your personal or health data after your account is deleted. We do not keep backups beyond our routine, short-term infrastructure backups, which are rotated out and fully purged within 30 days of deletion. We may retain minimal, non-identifying records (such as an anonymized log that a deletion occurred) as required to comply with legal obligations.

Trouble Deleting Your Account?

If the app is unable to complete the deletion (for example, due to a network issue), try again once you have a stable connection. The deletion only completes once you receive confirmation and are signed out.
