Delete Your Stedfast Account

Last updated: July 18, 2026

Stedfast is an intermittent fasting and macro tracking app developed by Shriram M S. This page explains how to request deletion of your Stedfast account and the data associated with it.

How to Request Deletion

To request that your Stedfast account and all associated data be deleted, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to dev.shriram.ms@gmail.com from the email address registered on your Stedfast account.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete My Stedfast Account" and include the email address associated with your account in the body of the message.
  3. We will verify the request and permanently delete your account and data. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.
Processing time: Deletion requests are processed within 7 days of receipt.

What Data Is Deleted

Requesting account deletion permanently removes:

  • Your account credentials (name, email address, password)
  • All logged fasting sessions and fasting schedules
  • All saved dishes, meals, and meal logs
  • All body stats (height, weight, and other measurements you logged)
  • Your calorie and macronutrient intake limits and daily intake summaries

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once processed, your data cannot be recovered.

What Data Is Retained

We do not retain any of your personal or health data after your account is deleted. We do not keep backups beyond our routine, short-term infrastructure backups, which are rotated out and fully purged within 30 days of your deletion request. We may retain minimal, non-identifying records (such as an anonymized log that a deletion request was fulfilled) as required to comply with legal obligations.
