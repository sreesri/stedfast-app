```
Stedfast — Intermittent Fasting & Macro Tracker

Stedfast helps you build a sustainable intermittent fasting routine and stay on top of your daily nutrition, without the clutter of a general-purpose fitness app.

FASTING, YOUR WAY
- Set a custom fasting-to-eating ratio (16:8, 18:6, 20:4, OMAD, or any window you choose) and a start time that fits your schedule.
- Track your current fast or eating window in real time from the home screen.
- Switch between fasting and eating with a single tap.

MEALS & MACROS
- Build a personal library of dishes with calories, protein, carbs, and fat.
- Save your regular meals as templates so logging takes seconds.
- Log what you eat throughout the day and see your running totals update instantly.
- Set daily calorie and macro targets, and see how close you are at a glance.

TRENDS
- View your intake and macros over daily, weekly, or monthly periods.
- Log your body weight and watch your trend over time.

SIMPLE BY DESIGN
Stedfast focuses on the two things that matter for fasting and nutrition: your eating window and what you eat in it. No ads, no third-party trackers, no noise.

Your data belongs to you. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings.
```
